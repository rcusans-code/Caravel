**What "lenders never hold the share" changes.** In every gated row it removes issuer approval for lenders and the fee treasury, because USDC is not KYC-gated (it is only revocable). The borrower is approved by definition, since it already holds the token. The only new approvals any issuer must give are the repo contract and the named liquidators. This puts Caravel level with XOXNO and Aave Horizon, not ahead of them. XOXNO's USDC suppliers are open too.

**Issuer powers reach Caravel's escrow.** Franklin can claw back or deauthorize through the SAC. Spiko can `pause`, de-whitelist, or `burn` from any holder. Any of these freezes repay, top-up and liquidation. The the SAFO branch tests `paused_token_freezes_repay_top_up_and_liquidate` and `borrower_removed_after_take_cannot_repay_but_lender_recovers` show this. So DECISIONS #6, "a borrower can always exit", holds only while the issuer allows it.

**XOXNO cofounder's claim, "fund access on XOXNO is under KYC": PARTLY TRUE.**
- True in code. A gated token's own allowlist does apply. Depositing, withdrawing and transfer-mode liquidation all need the pool, the holder and the liquidator allowlisted ([liqvid_rwa_collateral.rs#L503-L517](https://github.com/XOXNO/rs-lending-xlm/blob/30d59905/tests/test-harness/tests/controller/liqvid_rwa_collateral.rs#L503-L517)).
- But XOXNO runs no KYC itself. Its contracts contain no allowlist or KYC code: grepping contracts/, common/ and interfaces/ found only a swap-fee whitelist and a Blend-pool allowlist (controller/src/config/registry.rs#L36). The controller stays off the allowlist (#L432-L436), and USDC lenders are open. **Credit-mode liquidation gives a non-allowlisted liquidator the seized claim without any token transfer**; the issuer gate applies only when it withdraws ([#L527-L547](https://github.com/XOXNO/rs-lending-xlm/blob/30d59905/tests/test-harness/tests/controller/liqvid_rwa_collateral.rs#L527-L547)).
- On mainnet, no KYC-gated fund is live on XOXNO. All 6 Spiko markets are `enabled:false`, its pool `CBXRND…` and controller have no Spiko whitelist role (`has_role` returns null), and Liqvid is testnet-only. The fund collateral it does accept on mainnet (USDY, USTRY, CETES, deJ*) is ungated.

## Decisions for the team (default applies unless someone objects)

1. **How to pitch gating.** Default: say "lenders need no KYC; the issuer approves one contract and named liquidators, and a seized share only ever moves to an issuer-approved holder". Drop "a gap only Caravel can serve". XOXNO matches the lender side, and its credit-mode liquidation is the one real contrast.
2. **The Spiko ask.** One permission manager (`CBGHLRSX…`) covers USTBL, EUTBL, SAFO and eurSAFO. Default: one request, made by a team member, not by an agent, for `WLISTED` on the Caravel contract and the liquidators. List USTBL first at 3% (#26) and SAFO second.
3. **SAFO conflict.** DECISIONS #26 says "SAFO not enabled" until due diligence; issue #20 sets 3000 bps. Default: tSAFO enabled on testnet only at 3000 bps; mainnet SAFO stays disabled until due diligence, per #26.
4. **A real third-party token in the demo?** Default: no. Keep the tBENJI and tSAFO mocks. Etherfuse sandbox CETES is the only real option (open, 47 contracts already hold it), if someone wants it after the demo works.
5. **Issuer freeze vs. "borrower can always exit".** Default: add a README Risks line saying issuer pause, de-whitelisting or clawback freezes repay, top-up and liquidation. No contract change before the demo.
