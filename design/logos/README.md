# Logos

The Caravel mark is a complete C with a conquistador climbing it: helmet and cape, front foot planted on the C's lower tip, the back leg hidden. He carries a pole up past the top end of the C, and the flag flies out to the right, outside the letter. Three colors at most. The C, figure and flag are separate shapes, so each mono version still reads.

The artwork was traced from the hand-drawn sketch, so its edges are slightly irregular on purpose. It is the only rounded, organic shape in a system that is otherwise square.

The mark's frame is 177 × 128 (about 1.4 : 1) because the flag extends to the right of the C. Size it by height.

Rules:
- Minimum size: the mark 24px tall, the lockup 120px wide. For favicons at 16px, use the mono mark.
- Clear space: the height of the flag on every side.
- Three colors, never more: C, figure, flag. The wordmark reuses the C's russet.
- In the lockup, the wordmark sits under the flag. Never move it out from under the flag or put it on a second line.
- Never recolor parts individually, rotate, outline, add shadows or a double layer, or put the full-color mark on `primary` or `brand` fills; use the mono-white mark there.
- The wordmark is outlined, so it renders without the font installed.
